[1] Conversation — track 1, 3, 4
Jonah can't find his wallet. His friend Joanna helps him retrace his steps.
Joanna: OK, don't panic. When did you last see it?
Jonah: At lunch. I paid, so I **must have had** it then.
Joanna: Then you **might have left** it on the table.
Jonah: No, I **can't have left** it there. I put it right back in my jacket.
Joanna: What about the bus? It **could've slipped** out when you sat down.
Jonah: Maybe. Or someone **might have taken** it. It was so crowded.
Joanna: Wait a second. What's that in your back pocket?
Jonah: Oh, wow. I **must have put** it there without thinking. Don't tell anyone!

[2] Speak Like a Native — track 2
1. I paid, so I must have had it then.
change: “must have” (→ /ˈmʌstəv/)
strong: “paid”, “must”
“must have” đọc nhanh thành “mớs-tơv”: “have” chỉ còn /əv/, âm /t/ nối sang. Nhấn “paid” (bằng chứng) và “must” (chắc chắn); “then” đã rõ nên đọc nhẹ.

2. No, I can't have left it there.
change: “can't have” (→ /ˈkæntəv/)
linking: “left‿it”
strong: “can't”, “there”
Giọng Mỹ đọc “can't” với âm /æ/ mở rộng: “ken-tơv”; còn “can” chỉ là /kən/. “left it” nối thành “lép-tit”. Nhấn “can't”: phủ định chắc chắn.

3. It could've slipped out when you sat down.
weak: “could've”
linking: “slipped‿out”
change: “sat down” (mất âm t)
strong: “slipped”, “sat”
“could've” = could have, đọc “cu-đơv”. Đuôi -ed của “slipped” đọc /t/ và nối sang “out”: “slip-tao”. “sat down” mất âm /t/: “se-đaon”.

[MẸO PHÁT ÂM]
must have / might have / could've: “have” đọc nhẹ /əv/ → “mớs-tơv”, “mai-đơv”, “cu-đơv”.
Đuôi -ed sau /p/, /k/, /s/… đọc là /t/ và nối sang nguyên âm phía sau: slipped out → “slip-tao”.
Âm /t/ đứng trước phụ âm thường mất: sat down → “se-đaon”.
Khi suy đoán, nhấn vào modal để thể hiện độ chắc chắn: I MUST have…, I CAN'T have…

[3] Study Notes
**When did you last see it?**
Lần cuối bạn thấy nó là khi nào?
Câu hỏi quen thuộc khi tìm đồ thất lạc; chú ý “last” đứng trước động từ chính.
Cách nói tương đương: When was the last time you had it?

**I must have put it there without thinking.**
Chắc mình tiện tay bỏ vào đó mà không để ý.
“without thinking” = làm theo phản xạ, theo thói quen, không suy nghĩ.
Cách nói tương đương: I must have done it on autopilot.

[4] Grammar Focus
must have / can't have + V3: chắc chắn về quá khứ
must have + V3 = chắc chắn đã…; can't have (hoặc couldn't have) + V3 = chắc chắn đã không… Dùng khi có bằng chứng rõ ràng.
The lights are off. They [[must have gone]] to bed.
She [[can't have seen]] us. She didn't wave.
You [[must have been]] tired after the trip.

might / could have + V3: có lẽ đã (không chắc)
might / could have + V3 = có lẽ đã…, có thể đúng hoặc sai. Phủ định: might not have + V3 (có lẽ đã không…).
He's late. He [[might have missed]] the bus.
I [[could have left]] my umbrella at work.
Where's Tom? – He [[might have gone]] home.
