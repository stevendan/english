[1] Conversation — track 15, 17, 18
Tickets for Leah and Derek's favorite band went on sale this morning. Derek tried to buy two.
Leah: So? Please tell me we're going!
Derek: Bad news. I had two tickets in my cart, and then my card got declined.
Leah: No way! What happened?
Derek: My bank thought it was fraud. **If I'd used** my other card, we **would've been** fine.
Leah: Couldn't you just switch to the other one?
Derek: I tried, but they sold out in eight minutes!
Leah: Oh well. **If we'd gone**, **we'd have been** stuck way in the back anyway.
Derek: True. And I **wouldn't have heard** a thing over your singing.

[2] Speak Like a Native — track 16
1. If I'd used my other card, we would've been fine.
linking: “If‿I'd”
weak: “would've”
strong: “other card”, “fine”
“If I'd” nối thành “i-faid”, âm /d/ rất nhẹ nhưng không bỏ. “would've” = would have, đọc nhẹ /ˈwʊdəv/, nối vào “been”: “wu-đơv-bin”.

2. If we'd gone, we'd have been stuck way in the back anyway.
weak: “we'd have been”
strong: “stuck”, “way in the back”
“we'd” đầu là we had (theo sau là V3 “gone”); “we'd” sau là we would (theo sau là “have”). “we'd have been” lướt nhanh: “wi-đơv-bin”.

3. And I wouldn't have heard a thing over your singing.
change: “wouldn't have” (→ /ˈwʊdntəv/)
linking: “heard‿a”
strong: “thing”, “singing”
“wouldn't have” khi nói nhanh còn /ˈwʊdntəv/ “wu-đơn-tơv”. “heard a” nối thành “hơr-đơ”. Giọng trêu đùa, nhấn vào “singing”.

[MẸO PHÁT ÂM]
would've / could've / should've: “have” đọc nhẹ /əv/ → “wu-đơv”, “cu-đơv”, “shu-đơv”. Đừng viết thành “would of”.
'd + V3 = had (we'd gone); 'd + have hoặc động từ nguyên mẫu = would (we'd have been).
Âm /t/ gặp /j/ (you) biến thành “ch”: Couldn't you → “cu-đơn-chu”.
Câu tiếc nuối nhấn vào chi tiết “giá mà”: If I'd used my OTHER CARD…

[3] Study Notes
**Please tell me we're going!**
Làm ơn bảo là tụi mình được đi xem nhé!
“Please tell me + mệnh đề” = mình rất mong điều đó là thật (và hơi lo là không phải).
Cách nói tương đương: We're going, right? Say yes!

**My card got declined.**
Thẻ của mình bị từ chối thanh toán.
“get + V3” mang nghĩa bị động, rất hay dùng trong văn nói: got declined, got canceled.
Cách nói tương đương: My card didn't go through.

[4] Grammar Focus
Câu điều kiện loại 3: If + had + V3, would have + V3
Tưởng tượng một quá khứ khác với sự thật, thường để tiếc nuối hoặc trách móc. Khi nói: If I'd…, I would've / I'd have…
[[If]] she [[had studied]], she [[would have passed]].
[[If I'd known]], [[I would've]] called you.
We [[wouldn't have]] gotten lost [[if]] you'd listened.

Phủ định và cách nhận ra 'd
'd + V3 = had; 'd + have = would. Phủ định: If… hadn't + V3, … wouldn't have + V3.
[[If]] it [[hadn't rained]], [[we'd have]] gone hiking.
She [[wouldn't have]] come [[if]] you [[hadn't]] invited her.
