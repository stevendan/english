[2] Speak Like a Native — track 9
1. Can I help you with something?
smooth: “Can I”, “help you”
weak: “with”
Can I và help you đọc liền mạch như là một từ.

2. And do you know if they accept traveler's checks?
smooth: “do you know”
Chữ “d” trong từ and nối với chữ “d” trong từ do, nên 1 trong 2 cái bị lược bỏ đi.

3. I'm not really sure.
smooth: “I'm not”
strong: “not”
I'm not đọc liền mạch như là một từ. Ngoài ra, nhấn mạnh vào từ not.

[3] Study Notes

[4] Grammar Focus
Câu hỏi gián tiếp
Bạn có thể đặt một câu hỏi gián tiếp với cấu trúc “Do you know + if/whether/how/wh* + S + V?”
A: [[Do you know]] if Brian has a girlfriend?
B: Yeah, he does.

A: [[Do you know]] whether or not the manager is coming back?
B: Yes, I think she is.

A: [[Do you know]] how much it costs to fly to Chicago?
B: I'm not sure.

A: [[Do you know]] what time the grocery store opens?
B: Yes, it opens at 10 a.m.

[6] Substitution Drill — track 14
1. Do you know __if there's a drugstore around here__?
a. if there's a souvenir shop around here
b. whether there's a supermarket around here
c. whether or not there's a bookstore around here

2. Also, do you know __what time they close__?
a. which bus goes to the museum
b. where the nearest bank is
c. when they are open

3. Do you know __where I can get a new map__?

b. how long it takes to get to the airport
