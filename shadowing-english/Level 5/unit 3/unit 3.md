[1] Conversation — track 15, 17, 18
Charles and his granddaughter May are chatting on the phone.
Charles: Hi, May! Nice to hear from you. How's Germany?
May: Wonderful. I really **enjoy** liv**ing** in another country. It's a great experience!
Charles: I bet! Well, how's school going?
May: School's going great. Actually, I **wanted to** try for a scholarship ...
Charles: Oh, is that right?
May: Yeah, but in the end I **decided to** come home and look for a job.
Charles: I see. So when are you coming home?
May: Pretty soon. To be honest, I'm really **looking forward to** see**ing** you all again.

[2] Speak Like a Native — track 16
1. Actually, I wanted to try for a scholarship.
smooth: “wanted to”, “for a”
weak: “for a”
“Wanted to” và “for a” phát âm liền mạch như là 1 từ.

2. Yeah, but in the end I decided to come home and look for a job.
smooth: “but in the end”, “decided to”, “look for a job”
weak: “in the”, “to”, “for a”
strong: “decided”, “come”, “look”
“Ci” trong decided, come và look đọc mạnh lên.
In the, to, for a đọc nhẹ đi.

3. To be honest, I'm really looking forward to seeing you all again.
smooth: “To be honest”, “looking forward to”, “seeing you all again”
weak: “To be”, “to”
strong: “really”
Looking forward to và seeing you all again đọc liền mạch như một từ.

[3] Study Notes

[4] Grammar Focus
Động từ chỉ có thể theo sau bởi từ to
I [[want to]] be a soccer player.
Last summer, Andy [[decided to]] study in Spain.

Cụm từ chỉ có thể theo sau bởi V-ing
I used to [[enjoy]] driv[[ing]] along the coast.
I [[look forward to]] hear[[ing]] from you.
