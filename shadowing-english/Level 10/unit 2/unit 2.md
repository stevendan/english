[1] Conversation — track 8, 10, 11
Benedict and Celeste are getting married at a vineyard on Saturday. The nerves are kicking in.
Benedict: Bad news. It's **supposed to** bucket down on Saturday.
Celeste: Figures. Well, something was **bound to** go wrong. At least we've got the tent.
Benedict: True. And I've finished all the place cards. By hand.
Celeste: Oh, babe. Didn't I tell you? The venue prints them for free.
Benedict: You're kidding. So I **needn't have** stayed up till two?
Celeste: Oops, my bad. Hey, aren't you **supposed to** be writing your speech?
Benedict: Working on it. Your mum's **bound to** cry, whatever I say.
Celeste: She cries at commercials, so that's a given. Just keep it under five minutes.
Benedict: Five minutes? You're **supposed to** be on my side!
Celeste: I am. That's why I'm giving you a time limit.

[2] Speak Like a Native — track 9
1. You're kidding. So I needn't have stayed up till two?
change: “kidding” (i → ơ)
linking: “needn't‿have”, “stayed‿up”
strong: “kidding”, “needn't”, “two”
Giọng New Zealand thường đọc /ɪ/ gần như “ơ”: “cơ-đing”. “have” đọc yếu /əv/, mất /h/: “ní-đơn-tơv”. Lên giọng ở cuối vì bất ngờ.

2. Well, something was bound to go wrong.
change: “bound to” (mất âm d)
weak: “was”, “to”
strong: “bound”, “wrong”
“bound to” đọc thành “bao-ntơ”. Chữ w trong “wrong” câm: /rɔːŋ/. Xuống giọng ở cuối, kiểu chán nản: biết ngay mà.

3. Hey, aren't you supposed to be writing your speech?
change: “aren't‿you” (t + y → “ch”)
weak: “to”, “your”
strong: “supposed”, “speech”
“supposed to” đọc liền, mất /d/: “sơ-pâus-tơ”. Giọng Mỹ: “writing” → “rai-đing”. Câu hỏi phủ định để nhắc khéo nên xuống giọng.

[MẸO PHÁT ÂM]
Khi nói nhanh, /d/ giữa hai phụ âm thường mất: supposed to → “sơ-pâus-tơ”, bound to → “bao-ntơ”.
“have” sau needn't, should, could đọc yếu /əv/: needn't have → “ní-đơn-tơv”.
Giọng New Zealand thường đọc /ɪ/ gần “ơ” (kidding, fish); giọng Mỹ đọc t giữa hai nguyên âm thành “d” nhẹ.
Nhấn mạnh từ mang cảm xúc (needn't, two) để lộ vẻ tiếc công, ngạc nhiên.

[3] Study Notes
**It's supposed to bucket down on Saturday.**
Nghe nói thứ Bảy trời sẽ mưa như trút nước.
“bucket down” (Anh, Úc, New Zealand) = mưa rất to. “be supposed to” ở đây nghĩa là “nghe nói, theo dự báo”.
Cách nói tương đương: It's meant to pour on Saturday.

**She cries at commercials, so that's a given.**
Mẹ em xem quảng cáo còn khóc, nên chuyện đó khỏi phải bàn.
“that's a given” = điều hiển nhiên. “commercials” là từ Mỹ; người Anh hay nói “adverts”.
Cách nói tương đương: That goes without saying.

[4] Grammar Focus
be bound to / be supposed to + động từ nguyên mẫu
bound to: chắc chắn sẽ xảy ra. supposed to: lẽ ra phải (theo kế hoạch, quy định, vai trò), hoặc “nghe nói là”.
Don't worry, they're [[bound to]] love you.
The bus [[was supposed to]] come at nine.
This film [[is supposed to]] be brilliant.

needn't have + V3 / didn't need to + V
needn't have: đã làm nhưng hoá ra không cần (tiếc công). didn't need to: không cần làm, thường là đã không làm. Người Mỹ ít dùng needn't.
You [[needn't have brought]] wine. We've got plenty.
I [[didn't need to]] queue, so I walked straight in.
