[1] Conversation — track 15, 17, 18
Quynh is flying home to Vietnam for Tet. Her colleague Lionel is curious.
Lionel: So Tet is basically Vietnamese Christmas, right?
Quynh: Sort of. **It's a bit like** Christmas and New Year rolled into one.
Lionel: Okay, so what does everyone actually do?
Quynh: **Traditionally**, we clean the whole house beforehand. But during Tet itself, no sweeping.
Lionel: Wait, why not?
Quynh: **It's considered** bad luck. You'd be sweeping your good fortune out the door!
Lionel: Love it. And presents? We go a bit overboard at Christmas.
Quynh: Not so much. **It's customary to** give kids lucky money in red envelopes.
Lionel: Cash instead of socks? Those kids have it all figured out.
Quynh: Exactly! Oh, and the first guest of the year matters. **The idea is that** they set the tone.

[2] Speak Like a Native — track 16
1. It's a bit like Christmas and New Year rolled into one.
linking: “rolled‿into‿one”
weak: “a”, “and”
strong: “Christmas”, “New Year”, “one”
Chữ t trong “Christmas” câm: “kris-mơs”. “rolled into one” → “rôl-đin-tu-wăn”. Giọng New Zealand không đọc /r/ cuối “Year”: “yia”.

2. You'd be sweeping your good fortune out the door!
change: “You'd be” (mất âm d)
weak: “your”, “the”
strong: “sweeping”, “good fortune”, “door”
“fortune” /ˈfɔːtʃuːn/ đọc “fo-chun”. Giọng New Zealand không đọc /r/ trong “fortune”, “door”. Giọng vui, hơi cường điệu.

3. Cash instead of socks? Those kids have it all figured out.
linking: “instead‿of”, “have‿it‿all”, “figured‿out”
strong: “Cash”, “socks”, “figured out”
Giọng Mỹ: “have it all” → “ha-vi-đol” (t → d nhẹ), “figured out” → “fi-gyơr-đao”. Lên giọng ở “socks?” vì thích thú.

[MẸO PHÁT ÂM]
Chữ t câm trong Christmas, listen, castle: “kris-mơs”, “li-sần”, “ca-sồ”.
Giọng New Zealand không đọc /r/ cuối (year, door); giọng Mỹ đọc rõ (figured, overboard).
Khi giải thích phong tục, nói chậm và nhấn từ khoá: bad luck, lucky money, first guest.
Nối các từ ngắn thành cụm: rolled‿into‿one, have‿it‿all.

[3] Study Notes
**We go a bit overboard at Christmas.**
Nhà tôi mua sắm hơi quá tay vào dịp Giáng sinh.
“go overboard” = làm quá đà (mua sắm, trang trí, ăn uống…).
Cách nói tương đương: We tend to overdo it at Christmas.

**Those kids have it all figured out.**
Mấy đứa nhỏ đó đúng là biết tính.
“have it all figured out” = hiểu rõ, tính toán đâu ra đấy. Lionel nói đùa: tiền mặt hơn hẳn đôi tất.
Cách nói tương đương: Those kids are onto something.

[4] Grammar Focus
Mô tả phong tục: Traditionally, … / It's customary to + V
Traditionally = theo truyền thống; It's customary to + V = theo phong tục thì làm gì (trang trọng hơn “people usually…”).
[[Traditionally]], the whole family eats together on New Year's Eve.
[[It's customary to]] take your shoes off indoors.

Giải thích ý nghĩa: It's considered… / The idea is that… / It's a bit like…
It's considered + tính từ / danh từ: quan niệm chung. The idea is that: giải thích lý do. It's a bit like: so sánh với điều người nghe đã biết.
[[It's considered]] rude to point at people.
[[The idea is that]] red keeps bad luck away.
[[It's a bit like]] Thanksgiving, but longer.
