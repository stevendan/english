Andrea and her colleague Craig are talking in the break room.
Andrea: That was the longest presentation ever!
Craig: Tell me about it! I nodded off **during** Kathy's speech.
Andrea: Was it that boring?
Craig: No, I'm just worn out. I have to turn in a budget report **in** two days, and it's a lot of work.
Andrea: I see. Speaking of budgets, is anyone in accounting here today?

[2] Speak Like a Native — track 9
1. I nodded off during Kathy's speech.
smooth: “nodded off”
Nối từ nodded với từ off tiếp theo và đọc liền mạch như một từ.

2. Was it that boring?
smooth: “Was it”
strong: “that”
Từ that có nghĩa như là “đến thế hả”. Đọc nhấn mạnh từ that.

3. Jenny will be in the office until 3:00.
smooth: “Jenny will be”, “in the office”
“Jenny will be” đọc liền mạch giống như là “Jenny'll be”. Đọc nhấn mạnh từ office trong cụm in the office.

[4] Grammar Focus
Giới từ chỉ thời gian - thời điểm: during
Please keep your seat belt fastened [[during]] the flight.

Giới từ chỉ thời gian - thời điểm: for
I didn't see her [[for]] a long time.

Giới từ chỉ thời gian - thời điểm: until
I stayed at home [[until]] noon.

Giới từ chỉ thời gian - thời điểm: by
Please be ready to go [[by]] six o'clock.

Giới từ chỉ thời gian - thời điểm: in
We're going to move to the suburbs of Chicago [[in]] three weeks.
